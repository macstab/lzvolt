# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the code, not the person. Bring evidence.

## The longer version

This project is about measurable things. Most disagreements here have an
answer, and the way to settle one is a number, a disassembly, or a failing
test — not volume and not seniority. "I measured it" beats "it should be
faster", every time, and a measurement that contradicts a maintainer is a
contribution and not an affront.

Unacceptable, and it will be moderated:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including sexual attention that is not
  wanted.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

Acceptable, and welcome:

- Saying a change is wrong, and why.
- Saying you do not know.
- Reopening a settled question with new evidence.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/macstab/lzvolt/security/advisories/new),
which is the one private channel this repository has. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Responses range from a private word, through editing or removing a comment, to
a ban. What happens depends on severity and on whether it is a pattern.

Maintainers are held to this too. A maintainer who breaks it should expect to
hear about it, and the project is better if someone says so.
